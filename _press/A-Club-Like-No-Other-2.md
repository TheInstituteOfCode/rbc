CANGGU, BALI, JULY 2026 – Regent Bali Canggu proudly introduces Regent Ballroom, the only

luxury ballroom in the heart of Canggu, marking a new chapter for meetings, celebrations and

destination events on Bali's celebrated southwest coast.

As Canggu continues to evolve as one of Bali's most dynamic lifestyle destinations, Regent

Ballroom offers a sophisticated setting where contemporary design, intuitive service and

exceptional hospitality come together to create memorable occasions. From international

conferences and executive retreats to elegant weddings, gala dinners and milestone celebrations,

the venue has been thoughtfully designed to bring people together with effortless refinement.

Spanning 375 square metres, Regent Ballroom accommodates up to 400 guests for cocktail

receptions, 350 in theatre configuration, 250 for banquets and 80 for long-table dining. Flooded

with natural daylight, the space offers a warm and inviting atmosphere, complemented by

advanced event technology and the flexibility to accommodate gatherings of every scale.

The experience is further enhanced by Regent Salon I and Regent Salon II, two elegant meeting

spaces ideal for board meetings, private presentations, breakout sessions and more intimate

occasions. Together, the venues offer a seamless and versatile setting for both business and

social events.

At Regent Bali Canggu, every gathering extends beyond the event itself. Guests can discover the

resort's distinctive culinary destinations, from authentic Indonesian flavours at Taru and

Mediterranean-inspired cuisine at Sazón to contemporary European dining at Cure, alongside

beachfront celebrations at Beach House and bespoke experiences at Chef's Table. Each venue

offers its own unique setting, transforming meetings into occasions that continue well beyond the

final agenda.

For incentive groups and destination events, the experience continues through thoughtfully

curated activities inspired by Bali's rich culture and natural surroundings. Private yoga sessions,

surf experiences, cultural immersions and temple visits provide meaningful opportunities for

connection, while Regent Spa & Wellness invites guests to restore and recharge with holistic

treatments, vitality pools, sauna, cold plunge and wellness rituals designed to complement every

stay.

Accommodation has been thoughtfully designed with today's discerning traveller in mind.

Spacious suites, private pool villas and luxurious penthouses are complemented by Regent

Hallmarks, including all-day breakfast, a personalised Refreshment Gallery, unlimited laundry and

curated guest experiences. Guests staying in selected accommodation categories also enjoy

exclusive access to Regent Club, offering private check-in, all-day culinary offerings, premium

beverages and an oceanfront infinity pool.

Located within Canggu's vibrant coastal district, the resort places guests moments from world-

renowned surf breaks, acclaimed dining destinations, boutique shopping and Bali's thriving

wellness scene, allowing business and leisure to unfold effortlessly within a single destination.

"Regent Ballroom represents an exciting milestone in the continued evolution of Regent Bali

Canggu," said Manish Puri, General Manager of Regent Bali Canggu. "We envisioned a venue

that reflects the sophistication and warmth of the Regent experience while meeting the growing

demand for exceptional events in Canggu. Whether hosting a global conference, an intimate

celebration or a once-in-a-lifetime wedding, Regent Ballroom has been thoughtfully designed to

create meaningful moments that guests will remember long after the occasion."

With the introduction of Regent Ballroom, Regent Bali Canggu further strengthens its position as

one of Indonesia's leading luxury destinations. More than a venue, it offers a complete setting

where exceptional events, personalised hospitality and immersive resort experiences come

together, inviting guests to gather, celebrate and connect in one of Bali's most sought-after coastal

destinations.

For more information about Regent Ballroom or to enquire about meetings and events at

Regent Bali Canggu, please contact the Sales & Marketing team at

salesmarketing@regentbalicanggu.com.
